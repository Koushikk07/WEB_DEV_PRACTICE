Query operators

Find students where marks > 75

db.student.find({mark:{$gt:75}})

Find students who live in Nalgonda or Hyderabad

db.student.find({city:{$in:["Nalgonda","Hyderabad"]}})

Find students who scored > 75 or live in Nalgonda

db.student.find({$or:[{marks:{$gt:75}}.{city:"Nalgonda"}]})
